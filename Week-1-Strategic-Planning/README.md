# Week 1 – Strategic Planning and Data Exploration in Logistics

## Objective

This task focuses on the planning and strategy phase of a logistics data analysis project. The objective is to understand a logistics scenario, identify important Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), and determine how data science techniques can be used to improve supply chain efficiency and resource allocation.

## Logistics Scenario

The selected scenario focuses on a logistics delivery company that manages shipments across multiple regions.

The company collects operational data related to:

- Delivery distance
- Delivery time
- Expected delivery time
- Actual delivery time
- Delivery cost
- Package weight
- Vehicle type
- Delivery partner
- Weather conditions
- Delivery status
- Customer rating

## Business Problem

The logistics company wants to improve delivery performance while reducing operational costs.

The major challenges include:

1. Delivery delays
2. Increasing transportation costs
3. Inefficient resource allocation
4. Route inefficiencies
5. Poor visibility into delivery performance
6. Variations in customer satisfaction

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs can be used to measure logistics performance:

### 1. On-Time Delivery Rate

Measures the percentage of deliveries completed within the expected delivery time.

### 2. Average Delivery Time

Measures the average number of days required to complete a delivery.

### 3. Average Delivery Cost

Measures the average cost incurred per shipment.

### 4. Delivery Delay Rate

Measures the percentage of shipments that arrive later than expected.

### 5. Average Distance per Delivery

Helps understand transportation requirements and route efficiency.

### 6. Customer Satisfaction

Customer ratings can be used to evaluate the quality of the delivery service.

## Data Science Approach

Python-based data analysis can be used to:

- Clean and preprocess logistics data
- Identify missing values
- Detect outliers
- Analyze delivery patterns
- Visualize logistics KPIs
- Identify factors contributing to delivery delays
- Analyze transportation costs
- Build predictive models for delivery delays

## Proposed Technologies

The project will use:

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Scikit-learn
- Jupyter Notebook
- Power BI

## Expected Benefits

The proposed analytics solution can help the logistics company:

- Reduce delivery delays
- Improve route planning
- Reduce transportation costs
- Improve resource allocation
- Monitor logistics KPIs
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Support data-driven decision making

## Conclusion

A structured data analytics approach can help logistics companies transform operational data into useful business insights. By monitoring KPIs and applying data science techniques, the company can identify inefficiencies and improve overall supply chain performance.
